Week 7 - Assignment 7 (Questions with Correct Answers)

1. Non-formal education is defined as:

A. Organized learning outside schools
B. Learning through daily experiences
C. Classroom learning with fixed syllabus
D. Learning only within institutions

Correct Answer: A - Organized learning outside schools

2. Gamification in education means:

A. Playing games for entertainment
B. Avoiding tests during lessons
C. Applying game elements in learning
D. Using games instead of lessons

Correct Answer: C - Applying game elements in learning

3. One key difference between formal and non-formal education is:

A. Presence of trained teachers
B. Use of printed textbooks
C. Methods of evaluation used
D. Level of learner autonomy

Correct Answer: D - Level of learner autonomy

4. Digital libraries in ODL provide:

A. Storage of printed books
B. Distribution of printed notes
C. Access to online resources
D. Provision of classroom furniture

Correct Answer: C - Access to online resources

5. Platforms such as SWAYAM and Coursera primarily support:

A. Credential-based formal programs
B. Informal and lifelong learning
C. Therapeutic support services
D. Compulsory school education

Correct Answer: B - Informal and lifelong learning

6. A developmental portfolio is best characterized by:

A. Storage of institutional records
B. Reporting of standardized grades
C. Presentation of final products
D. Continuous documentation of progress

Correct Answer: D - Continuous documentation of progress

7. One major advantage of online quizzes in LMS platforms is:

A. Automated feedback and analytics
B. Requirement of manual grading
C. Reduction of learner engagement
D. Limitation of question formats

Correct Answer: A - Automated feedback and analytics

8. Virtual Reality (VR) differs from AR primarily because VR:

A. Depends on printed learning materials
B. Uses mobile devices for display
C. Provides fully immersive environments
D. Cannot be applied in education

Correct Answer: C - Provides fully immersive environments

9. Extended Reality (XR) is a term used to describe:

A. Artificial intelligence platforms
B. Only virtual reality environments
C. Only augmented reality systems
D. Spectrum of immersive technologies

Correct Answer: D - Spectrum of immersive technologies

10. 360 degree immersive video differs from true VR because it:

A. Lacks full environmental immersion
B. Cannot display motion images
C. Requires no visual display
D. Is unrelated to virtual reality

Correct Answer: A - Lacks full environmental immersion
