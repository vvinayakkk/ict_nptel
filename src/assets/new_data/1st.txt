Q1

Which of the following is a core component of Educational Technology?

A. Classroom discipline
B. Hardware resources
C. Moral instruction
D. Examination schedules

✅ Official Answer: B — Hardware resources

Q2

NEP 2020 emphasizes which mode to handle future educational disruptions?

A. Exclusive classroom teaching
B. Complete distance education
C. Online and digital education
D. Informal community learning

✅ Official Answer: C — Online and digital education

Q3

Programmed instruction in the early 20th century was primarily influenced by:

A. Cognitive learning theory
B. Behaviourist learning theory
C. Constructivist learning theory
D. Humanistic learning theory

✅ Official Answer: B — Behaviourist learning theory

Q4

The learner-centred nature of Educational Technology mainly emphasizes:

A. Teacher authority in instruction
B. Uniform curriculum delivery
C. Centralized classroom control
D. Individual learner needs and styles

✅ Official Answer: D — Individual learner needs and styles

Q5

Asynchronous learning mainly allows learners to:

A. Learn at their own pace and schedule
B. Interact only in real time
C. Depend on classroom instruction
D. Receive immediate verbal feedback

✅ Official Answer: A — Learn at their own pace and schedule

Q6

Experiential instructional strategies primarily emphasize:

A. Learning through textbooks
B. Learning through lectures
C. Learning through direct experience
D. Learning through examinations

✅ Official Answer: C — Learning through direct experience

Q7

Interactive media such as simulations and educational games mainly support:

A. Passive content delivery
B. Administrative efficiency
C. Traditional examination systems
D. Active learner engagement

✅ Official Answer: D — Active learner engagement

Q8

Group discussions, debates, and peer teaching are examples of:

A. Direct instructional strategies
B. Indirect instructional strategies
C. Interactive instructional strategies
D. Independent instructional strategies

✅ Official Answer: C — Interactive instructional strategies

Q9

Adaptive learning technologies primarily focus on:

A. Uniform curriculum delivery
B. Personalized learning pathways
C. Group-based instruction
D. Teacher-centered assessment

✅ Official Answer: B — Personalized learning pathways

Q10

Gamification in education primarily uses:

A. Game elements to motivate learner
B. Printed worksheets and manuals
C. Teacher-led lecture methods
D. Traditional examination systems

✅ Official Answer: A — Game elements to motivate learner
