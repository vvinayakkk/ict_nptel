Q1

NIEPA mainly focuses on:

A. Medical education training
B. Educational planning, management
C. Mechanical engineering studies
D. Software programming courses

✅ Official Answer: C — Educational planning, management

Q2

IGNOU contributes to teacher education mainly through:

A. Distance learning programmes
B. Laboratory training courses
C. School inspection services
D. Curriculum printing services

✅ Official Answer: C — School inspection services

Q3

Continuous Professional Development (CPD) means:

A. One-time training programme
B. Annual examination system
C. Fixed curriculum training
D. Ongoing professional learning

✅ Official Answer: B — Annual examination system

Q4

Immediate response analysis in CAI helps in:

A. Delaying learner feedback
B. Correcting learner errors
C. Skipping difficult lessons
D. Avoiding performance review

✅ Official Answer: A — Delaying learner feedback

Q5

Behaviorism supports CAI through:

A. Observational learning only
B. Memorization without feedback
C. Reinforcement of responses
D. Lecture-based instruction

✅ Official Answer: D — Lecture-based instruction

Q6

CML systems mainly depend on:

A. Student record databases
B. Chalkboard teaching methods
C. Printed record registers
D. Manual typewriter entries

✅ Official Answer: B — Chalkboard teaching methods

Q7

The Personalized System of Instruction (PSI) was developed by:

A. Fred S. Keller
B. B. F. Skinner
C. Robert Gagné
D. Benjamin Bloom

✅ Official Answer: C — Robert Gagné

Q8

In the Indian context, flipped classrooms align well with:

A. Gurukul system
B. e-learning platforms
C. Traditional chalk-and-talk

Q9

Using YouTube tutorials to learn skills is an example of:

A. Formal mobile learning
B. Work-based learning

Q10
