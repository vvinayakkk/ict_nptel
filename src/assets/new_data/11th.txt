Q1

DIKSHA portal is primarily designed for:

A. Banking skill training
B. Teacher learning resources
C. Industrial training courses
D. Engineering laboratory work

✅ Official Answer: C — Industrial training courses

Q2

NROER stands for:

A. National Research of Engineering
B. National Register of Exams
C. National Repository of OER
D. Network Resource of Education

✅ Official Answer: D — Network Resource of Education

Q3

Virtual classrooms mainly enable:

A. Printed lesson distribution

C. Offline record keeping

Q4

Speech-to-text technology converts:

A. Images into text
